NEMESIS brainstorming meeting

May 21, 2024 [Alberto Alesina Seminar Room, Bocconi University, Milan]

This meeting aims at creating the bases for a fruitful dialogue with quantitative criminology experts in order to discuss relevant data, ongoing projects, open problems in criminal network analysis, and possible future collaborations within project NEMESIS.

Program of the meeting

Time Description
9.30am – 11.00am Criminal networks (data, open problems and research questions in criminology)
11.00am – 1.00pm Future directions in modeling of criminal networks from a statistical and criminological perspective
1.00pm – 2.00pm Brainstorming/networking lunch

Participants

Alberto Aziani, Emanuele Aliverti, Francesco Calderoni, Matei-Gabriel Cosa, Daniele Durante, Serena Favarin, Beatrice Franzolini, Valeria Gerosa, Emanuele Moscato, Mirko Nazzari, Omiros Papaspiliopoulos, Tommaso Rigon.
